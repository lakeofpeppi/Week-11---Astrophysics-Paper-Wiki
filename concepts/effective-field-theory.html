Effective field theory

A way to describe physics using only the terms that matter at the scales you can measure.

Plain explanation

Instead of guessing the one true theory, you write down every term allowed by the symmetries, each with an unknown coefficient. Measurements then tell you the size of each coefficient.

For dark energy, this means you can test whole families of models at once. A coefficient like M₂⁴ controls one specific behaviour (here, how the field moves, its kinetic term).

Analogy: Like fitting a model's weights in ML without knowing the true data-generating process. Or like describing water with density and viscosity without tracking every molecule.

Why astronomers care

  • It's model-independent, so one measurement constrains many theories at once.
  • The coefficient values hint at what deeper (UV-complete) theory might be underneath.
