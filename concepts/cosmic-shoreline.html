Cosmic Shoreline

A proposed dividing line between planets that keep their atmospheres and planets that lose them.

Plain explanation

Plot planets by how strongly gravity holds onto gas (escape velocity) against how much high-energy radiation they've received. The Shoreline is the line between the two groups.

On the “wet” side atmospheres should survive; on the “dry” side they should be stripped. It works well in our Solar System, and JWST is testing it for exoplanets.

Analogy: A beach: on one side the tide (radiation) washes everything away, and on the other side things stay put.

Why astronomers care

  • It predicts which rocky exoplanets are worth searching for atmospheres.
  • It's being tested planet by planet with JWST.
