Sound speed (of dark energy)

How fast pressure waves move through dark energy, which decides whether it can clump.

Plain explanation

Written as cs², in units where the speed of light is 1. If cs² = 1, pressure spreads instantly-ish and dark energy stays smooth (like the standard ΛCDM picture).

If cs² ≪ 1, pressure can't keep up on large scales, so dark energy can clump on scales bigger than the so-called Jeans scale.

Analogy: Stiff jelly vs. runny honey. Poke stiff jelly and the dent springs back fast; slow material lets lumps form.

Why astronomers care

  • Clumping dark energy would rule out the simplest (cosmological constant) explanation.
  • It affects how galaxies and gravitational potentials grow over time.
