JWST

The James Webb Space Telescope: a 6.5 m infrared space telescope launched in 2021.

Plain explanation

JWST observes mainly in infrared light, which is ideal for cool objects (planets, brown dwarfs) and for distant galaxies.

It has several instruments: MIRI for mid-infrared, NIRISS and NIRSpec for near-infrared spectra, and more. Each paper usually says which one it used.

Analogy: A heat-sensing camera in space, sensitive enough to feel the warmth of a planet next to a bright star.

Why astronomers care

  • It can measure exoplanet atmospheres and temperatures directly.
  • Infrared shows objects too cool to shine in visible light.
