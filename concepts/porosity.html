Porosity

The fraction of a material's volume that is empty space.

Plain explanation

Written as φ, from 0 to 1. φ = 0 is completely solid, and φ = 0.8 means 80% of the volume is empty gaps between grains.

More empty space means fewer contact points for heat to pass through and less mass to store heat, so very porous materials are good insulators.

Analogy: A sugar cube vs. cotton candy: the same sugar, but cotton candy is mostly air.

Why astronomers care

  • It controls how heat flows through a surface, and so how its temperature behaves.
  • It decides how firm the ground is for a lander, and how much gas can get trapped inside.
