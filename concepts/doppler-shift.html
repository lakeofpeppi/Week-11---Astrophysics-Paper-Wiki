Doppler shift

Light from something moving toward you looks bluer; moving away, it looks redder.

Plain explanation

When a source of light moves toward you, its waves get squished and the light shifts to shorter, bluer wavelengths. Moving away, the waves stretch and shift redder. For a spinning ring of gas, one side comes toward you and the other side goes away, so a single emission line splits into two peaks. Two rings spinning at different speeds give four peaks.

Analogy: An ambulance siren sounds higher as it drives toward you and lower as it drives away.

Why astronomers care

  • It lets astronomers measure speeds from far away, just from a spectrum.
  • The shape of a line shows how gas is moving, for example in a rotating disc.
