BRDF

Bidirectional reflectance distribution function: how a surface reflects light for every incoming and outgoing direction.

Plain explanation

A BRDF is a function f(light direction, view direction) that tells you how much light a surface sends toward you.

A Lambertian surface (matte paper) scatters light evenly in all directions. A specular surface (polished metal) sends most light in one mirror direction. The Phong model mixes both with a simple formula.

Analogy: This is the same idea used in 3D graphics and game shaders. Satellite brightness models basically “render” the satellite from the telescope's point of view.

Why astronomers care

  • Satellite brightness depends heavily on what each surface is made of and its angle to the light.
  • A wrong BRDF means a wrong brightness prediction, even if the orbit is perfect.
