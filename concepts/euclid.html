Euclid

An ESA space telescope that maps a huge part of the sky in visible and near-infrared light.

Plain explanation

Euclid's main goal is mapping dark matter and dark energy through billions of galaxies. Its wide, sharp images also catch huge numbers of stars and brown dwarfs.

It takes images through several filters (IE, YE, JE, HE) and low-resolution grism spectra.

Analogy: A survey drone photographing a whole country in high resolution: built to map cities, but it catches everything else too.

Why astronomers care

  • Its data releases contain far more faint red objects than earlier surveys.
  • It pairs well with SPHEREx, which takes spectra across the whole sky.
