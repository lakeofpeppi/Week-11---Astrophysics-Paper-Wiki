How this wiki grows

rules live in CLAUDE.md

Drop in a paper

I give the AI an arXiv link. It's logged in raw/sources.md, and the PDF itself is never uploaded.

Paper page

Same 12 sections every time, and every number cites its section, figure or table.

Concepts update

Existing concepts get a new “appears in” row. New ones get their own page.

Conflicts flagged

If papers disagree, or a paper contradicts itself, it's marked “to verify”, never guessed.
