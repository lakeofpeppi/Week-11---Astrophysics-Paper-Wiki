## Concepts

- `concepts/apparent-magnitude.html` — Apparent magnitude: How bright an object looks from Earth, on a backwards, logarithmic scale. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites, 2026-lodieu-red-l-dwarfs
- `concepts/aurora.html` — Aurora: Light produced when charged particles crash into an atmosphere along magnetic field lines, like the northern lights. — used in: 2026-pike-ultracool-dwarf-aurora
- `concepts/brdf.html` — BRDF: Bidirectional reflectance distribution function: how a surface reflects light for every incoming and outgoing direction. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/cosmic-shoreline.html` — Cosmic Shoreline: A proposed dividing line between planets that keep their atmospheres and planets that lose them. — used in: 2026-ih-ltt1445-rocky-planets
- `concepts/dark-energy.html` — Dark energy: The unknown thing that makes the universe's expansion speed up. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/effective-field-theory.html` — Effective field theory: A way to describe physics using only the terms that matter at the scales you can measure. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/euclid.html` — Euclid: An ESA space telescope that maps a huge part of the sky in visible and near-infrared light. — used in: 2026-lodieu-red-l-dwarfs
- `concepts/exoplanet-atmosphere.html` — Exoplanet atmosphere: The gas layer around a planet outside our Solar System, if it has one. — used in: 2026-ih-ltt1445-rocky-planets, 2026-lodieu-red-l-dwarfs
- `concepts/fisher-matrix.html` — Fisher matrix: A quick way to forecast how precisely a future experiment could measure some parameters. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/free-floating-planet.html` — Free-floating planet: A planet-mass object drifting through space without orbiting any star. — used in: 2026-nagpal-wide-binary-planets, 2026-lodieu-red-l-dwarfs
- `concepts/galactic-tide.html` — Galactic tide: The stretching pull the whole Milky Way exerts on a system that is spread out over a large distance. — used in: 2026-nagpal-wide-binary-planets
- `concepts/hot-jupiter.html` — Hot Jupiter: A giant gas planet orbiting extremely close to its star, often in just a few days. — used in: 2026-nagpal-wide-binary-planets
- `concepts/icy-moon.html` — Icy moon: A moon whose surface is mostly water ice, like Europa, Ganymede and Callisto (Jupiter) or Enceladus (Saturn). — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/jwst.html` — JWST: The James Webb Space Telescope: a 6.5 m infrared space telescope launched in 2021. — used in: 2026-ih-ltt1445-rocky-planets, 2026-pike-ultracool-dwarf-aurora
- `concepts/lila.html` — LILA: Lunar Interferometer for Laser Astronomy: a proposed laser detector on the Moon. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/low-earth-orbit.html` — Low Earth orbit: Orbits close to Earth, below about 2,000 km. Home of the ISS and most satellite constellations. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/lunar-earthshine.html` — Lunar-Earthshine: Moonlight that bounces off Earth and lights up the Earth-facing side of a satellite. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/m-dwarf.html` — M dwarf: The smallest, coolest and most common type of hydrogen-burning star: a red dwarf. — used in: 2026-ih-ltt1445-rocky-planets, 2026-pike-ultracool-dwarf-aurora
- `concepts/moon.html` — The Moon: Earth's natural satellite. In this wiki, it's both a light source and a place to build a detector. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites, 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon, 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/orbital-eccentricity.html` — Orbital eccentricity: How stretched an orbit is, from 0 (a perfect circle) to almost 1 (a very long, thin oval). — used in: 2026-nagpal-wide-binary-planets
- `concepts/porosity.html` — Porosity: The fraction of a material's volume that is empty space. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/principal-component-analysis.html` — Principal component analysis: A method that finds the few main patterns that explain most of the variation in a dataset. — used in: 2026-pike-ultracool-dwarf-aurora
- `concepts/regolith.html` — Regolith: The loose layer of dust, grains or broken material covering a solid surface. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/rubin-observatory.html` — Rubin Observatory: The Vera C. Rubin Observatory: a large survey telescope in Chile that photographs the whole southern sky every few nights. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/secondary-eclipse.html` — Secondary eclipse: When a planet passes behind its star, so its own light briefly disappears from the total. — used in: 2026-ih-ltt1445-rocky-planets
- `concepts/sound-speed.html` — Sound speed (of dark energy): How fast pressure waves move through dark energy, which decides whether it can clump. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/surface-brightness.html` — Surface brightness: Brightness per patch of sky, measured in magnitudes per square arcsecond (mag/arcsec²). — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/thermal-inertia.html` — Thermal inertia: How strongly a surface resists changing its temperature. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/thermal-skin-depth.html` — Thermal skin depth: How deep a temperature change reaches into a surface during one heating and cooling cycle. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/ultracool-dwarf.html` — Ultracool dwarf: The coolest stars and brown dwarfs, spectral type M7 and later (L, T, Y), colder than about 2500 K. — used in: 2026-lodieu-red-l-dwarfs, 2026-pike-ultracool-dwarf-aurora
- `concepts/wide-binary.html` — Wide binary: Two stars orbiting each other from very far apart, here 1,000 to 100,000 times the Earth–Sun distance. — used in: 2026-nagpal-wide-binary-planets
