## Concepts

- `concepts/apparent-magnitude.html` — Apparent magnitude: How bright an object looks from Earth, on a backwards, logarithmic scale. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/brdf.html` — BRDF: Bidirectional reflectance distribution function: how a surface reflects light for every incoming and outgoing direction. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/dark-energy.html` — Dark energy: The unknown thing that makes the universe's expansion speed up. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/effective-field-theory.html` — Effective field theory: A way to describe physics using only the terms that matter at the scales you can measure. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/fisher-matrix.html` — Fisher matrix: A quick way to forecast how precisely a future experiment could measure some parameters. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/icy-moon.html` — Icy moon: A moon whose surface is mostly water ice, like Europa, Ganymede and Callisto (Jupiter) or Enceladus (Saturn). — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/lila.html` — LILA: Lunar Interferometer for Laser Astronomy: a proposed laser detector on the Moon. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/low-earth-orbit.html` — Low Earth orbit: Orbits close to Earth, below about 2,000 km. Home of the ISS and most satellite constellations. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/lunar-earthshine.html` — Lunar-Earthshine: Moonlight that bounces off Earth and lights up the Earth-facing side of a satellite. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/moon.html` — The Moon: Earth's natural satellite. In this wiki, it's both a light source and a place to build a detector. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites, 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon, 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/porosity.html` — Porosity: The fraction of a material's volume that is empty space. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/regolith.html` — Regolith: The loose layer of dust, grains or broken material covering a solid surface. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/rubin-observatory.html` — Rubin Observatory: The Vera C. Rubin Observatory: a large survey telescope in Chile that photographs the whole southern sky every few nights. — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/sound-speed.html` — Sound speed (of dark energy): How fast pressure waves move through dark energy, which decides whether it can clump. — used in: 2026-gurrola-dark-energy-moon
- `concepts/surface-brightness.html` — Surface brightness: Brightness per patch of sky, measured in magnitudes per square arcsecond (mag/arcsec²). — used in: 2026-caddy-moonlit-satellites
- `concepts/thermal-inertia.html` — Thermal inertia: How strongly a surface resists changing its temperature. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
- `concepts/thermal-skin-depth.html` — Thermal skin depth: How deep a temperature change reaches into a surface during one heating and cooling cycle. — used in: 2026-mergny-icy-moon-regolith
